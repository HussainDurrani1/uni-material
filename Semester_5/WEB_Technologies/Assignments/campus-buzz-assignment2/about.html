About Us

About CampusBuzz and the team behind it.

What is CampusBuzz?

CampusBuzz is a campus event platform for the University of Central Punjab (UCP). At UCP, societies and clubs organise many events every semester – tech workshops, cultural nights, sports tournaments, seminars, book fairs and career fairs – but students often find out about them too late, through random posters or group chats.

CampusBuzz solves this by putting all campus events in one place. Students can browse events by category, see the date, time and venue for each event, and plan which events they want to attend.

Why We Built It

This website is our Web Technologies semester project. Our teacher divided the semester project into 4 assignments that combine into one final project at the end of the semester. Assignment 1 made the base of the project: a Home page, an About Us page, a Contact page, and an Events List page.

Assignment 2 builds on the same design and adds Bootstrap, animations, and an event detail page. It also adds a My Schedule feature: CampusBuzz is about attending events, not buying products, so students add events to their own schedule to plan which ones to attend.
