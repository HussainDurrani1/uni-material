About CampusBuzz

Meet the project, its purpose, and the team behind it.

📅 What is CampusBuzz?

CampusBuzz is a campus event platform for the University of Central Punjab (UCP). It brings all campus events in one place — from cultural nights, sports tournaments and seminars to book fairs and career fairs.

Societies and clubs organise many events every semester, but students often find out about them too late, through random posters or group chats. CampusBuzz solves this: students can browse events by category, see the date, time and venue for each event, and save the events they want to attend in their own schedule.

🎯 Why We Built It?

This website is our Web Technologies semester project. Our teacher divided the semester project into 4 assignments that combine into one final project at the end of the semester.

Assignment 1 made the base of the project: a Home page, an About Us page, a Contact page, and an Events List page. Assignment 2 builds on the same design and adds Bootstrap, animations, an event detail page, and the My Schedule feature.
